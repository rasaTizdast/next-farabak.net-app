## Breadcrumbs and sitemap

The candidate keeps one structured trail per navigation path while preserving the visible RTL breadcrumb navigation. Listing pages use their `CollectionPage` breadcrumb as the owner; the visual breadcrumb component suppresses its duplicate JSON-LD there. Search explicitly opts into one trail. Product and article templates keep their own trail.

Verified candidate trails include:

- X-ray category: `خانه` → `محصولات` → `دستگاه ایکس ری` (positions 1–3).
- Airport-luggage subcategory: the X-ray category plus `خرید دستگاه ایکس ری فرودگاه و چمدانی` (positions 1–4).
- Page 2: the subcategory trail plus `صفحه 2` pointing to its HTTPS page-2 URL (positions 1–5).
- Blackmagic and Reolink products: home → products → category → subcategory (positions 1–4).
- Reolink article: home → support → blog → Reolink → article title (positions 1–5).

All candidate breadcrumb item URLs use `https://farabak.net`; positions are consecutive and labels are readable. Tests cover the collection schema, page-2 item, rendered visible navigation, and canonical origin.

Production `/sitemap.xml` on Nov 19 returned 482 HTTPS `<loc>` values and 482 `<lastmod>` entries generated within five timestamps during the same sitemap build. The candidate returned the same 482 HTTPS URLs and zero `<lastmod>` entries. Neither primary nor fallback entries claim an update time without a URL-specific source.

## Product snippets, price units, and Merchant listings

The Nov 15 Product snippets report supplied for this task listed the three product routes above as invalid for missing `offers`, `review`, or `aggregateRating`. The Nov 16 Merchant listings report had zero valid and zero invalid items. Current report status is pending Search Console access.

The public product flow does not confirm an online purchase checkout:

- HI-SCAN 6040 DV has no positive catalog price and directs visitors to contact sales. It remains quote-only and has no Offer.
- HyperDeck Shuttle 4K Pro and DeckLink Mini Recorder HD store a USD amount and show an initial price-loading message. Their UI retrieves a live exchange rate, displays an estimated Toman amount, and adds products to an invoice after login. No public payment step or stable ISO-currency checkout amount was verified.
- B400 is the representative positive-price, invoice-eligible product. Its USD catalog amount is converted with the same live Toman rate for display and invoice creation. The flow still ends in an invoice, not a verified online payment checkout, so it is not a confirmed Merchant listing product.

The exchange-rate tests pin the current units: BRS values are already Toman despite an upstream Rial unit label; PriceToDay values in Rial are divided by ten; Jahankhahan values are already Toman. The product schema therefore includes no numeric price or `priceCurrency` claim. No stock, availability, review, rating, shipping, return or offer details were invented. No eligible online-purchase product was confirmed; Merchant listing restoration remains pending verified catalog and checkout data. See Google’s [Product snippet](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product-snippet) and [Merchant listing](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/merchant-listing) requirements.

Rich Results Test and URL Inspection were not available from the disconnected Google account in this session. Validate the three products and one confirmed purchasable product with Google after verified purchase data exists and the deployment is live. Do not request Search Console validation before then.
